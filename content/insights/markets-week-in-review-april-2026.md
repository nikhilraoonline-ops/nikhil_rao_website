A constructive week for global markets, with equities leading the charge amid solid corporate earnings and a more accommodative central bank stance. Below is a structured recap of the key macro and market narratives from the week of April 21–25, 2026.

---

## The Week's Key Themes

1. **Q1 earnings beat expectations** — tech giants drove the rally with better-than-expected results
2. **Fed signals patience** — Powell's comments reinforced the 'higher for longer' narrative but with a dovish tilt
3. **Geopolitical de-escalation** — reduced tensions in key regions supported risk appetite
4. **Yield curve steepening** — long-end yields fell further as recession fears receded
5. **Emerging markets outperformance** — NIFTY 50 and other EM indices benefited from global liquidity

---

## Equity Markets

Global equities posted solid gains, with the S&P 500 up 1.12% and the NASDAQ surging 1.67%. Technology stocks led the charge, with AI-related companies reporting robust earnings growth. The NIFTY 50 also advanced 0.45%, supported by strong domestic economic data and foreign investor inflows.

European markets were mixed, with the DAX gaining 0.92% while the FTSE 100 dipped slightly due to UK-specific concerns.

---

## Fixed Income & Rates

Bond yields continued their decline, with the 10Y UST falling 13bps to 4.15%. The 2Y UST also dropped 15bps. This movement reflects growing confidence in a soft landing scenario and reduced immediate rate hike expectations.

The Fed Funds rate remained unchanged at 5.25–5.50%, with Chairman Powell emphasizing data dependency in future decisions.

---

## FX Developments

The USD weakened against major currencies, with EUR/USD climbing to 1.0915 (+0.67%). GBP/USD also rose 0.31% amid positive UK economic indicators. USD/INR declined to 83.12, reflecting improved risk sentiment and capital inflows.

USD/JPY fell to 148.47 as Japanese authorities intervened to prevent excessive yen strength.

---

## Commodities & Alternatives

Gold prices hit new highs, reaching $2,712 (+2.45%) on safe-haven demand despite the risk-on environment. WTI oil prices declined 3.12% to $68.89 amid concerns over global demand.

Bitcoin continued its upward trajectory, surpassing $102,850 (+6.42%) as institutional adoption increased.

---

## Market Outlook

The coming weeks should see continued focus on Q1 earnings and central bank communications. With the Fed signaling patience, markets may remain supportive of risk assets unless unexpected economic data emerges.

Key risks include potential flare-ups in geopolitical tensions and any signs of inflation resurgence.
